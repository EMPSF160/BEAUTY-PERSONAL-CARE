Circadian Framework

The 24-Hour Daylight Skin Rhythm

Cosmetic actives must be introduced in alignment with biological circadian shifts. Aligning with your natural biorhythm increases active bioavailability by over 300%.

06:00 AM – 11:00 AM

Morning Shield & Antioxidants

Skin permeability is lowest; epidermal sebum begins ascending. Focus on aqueous antioxidants (L-Ascorbic Acid, Ectoin) followed by broad-spectrum Zinc Oxide SPF 50+.

12:00 PM – 05:00 PM

High Noon Hydration & Defense

Sebaceous gland activity reaches its daily apex. Counteract dry air-conditioned offices with misted humectants and reapply mineral SPF powder.

08:00 PM – 02:00 AM

Night Mitosis & Lipid Repair

Blood flow to the dermis surges; TEWL peaks. The ideal window for encapsulated Retinaldehyde, Copper Peptides, and 3:1:1 lipid replenishment creams.
